## The short answers

**Is this more favorable than what Through Line had?**  
Yes — but not because we found more customers. Through Line never priced the ramp. It only counted logos. At the old concept price ($299 / agent / month), 48 agencies was about **$170k** of annual run-rate. At Monday’s $699 + $18 shopping, those same 48 agencies are about **$850k**. Same logos. About 5× the revenue.

**How quickly can we hit $1M?**  
You have to pick what “$1M” means.

| What you mean | How many agencies | Fastest honest date on our ramps |
|---|---|---|
| **$1M ARR** — if every current customer stays, we would collect $1M over the next 12 months | **57 agencies** | **June 2027** on the aggressive ramp, **July 2027** on the baseline (fundraising) ramp |
| **$1M in the bank** — cash actually collected | More than 57, because early logos have not paid for a full year yet | **December 2027** aggressive, **January 2028** baseline |

Through Line’s published ramp **stops in June 2027**. Baseline ends at 48 agencies — **just short** of $1M ARR. Aggressive ends at 60 — **just over**. So $1M ARR is “the next page after Path to Scale,” not a different business.

## 2. What “this model” is

Monday’s path, applied to Austin’s typical agency (the PDF he walked):

- **1,500 households**, 80% personal lines → 1,200 accounts, $4,000 average premium, $4.8M personal-lines book  
- **$699 / month** software  
- **$18** each time Upline shops (Members 1st: 43% of renewals complete a questionnaire → **43 shops / month**)  
- Life and AMS **not** in the revenue number  

Per agency, per year:

- Subscription: $699 × 12 = **$8,388**  
- Shopping: 43 × 12 × $18 = **$9,288**  
- **ARPU = $17,676** (~$1,473 / month)

$1,000,000 ÷ $17,676 = **56.6 → 57 agencies** for $1M ARR.

If they bring their own VAs and we never collect shopping, ARPU is only $8,388 and $1M ARR needs **119 agencies**. That is the fork that matters more than $699 vs $899.

Austin’s sheet also shows **$499** on the cost row. That is the *life-discounted internal case*, not the offer. Do not use $499 when you talk about time to $1M.

## 3. ARR vs cash (read this once)

**ARR** (annual run-rate) = agencies this month × $17,676.  
It answers: “If we froze the book right now, what would the next year bill?”  
Investors and TAM conversations use this.

**Cash collected** = the sum of monthly bills so far.  
An agency that starts in May has paid two months by June, not $17k.  
So cash always lags ARR.

Example, baseline ramp, June 2027:

- 48 agencies × $17,676 = **$848k ARR** (almost $1M)  
- Cash collected December–June ≈ **$220k**

Both numbers are true. They answer different questions. “When do we hit $1M?” almost always means ARR unless someone is talking about payroll.

## 5. So… is it more favorable?

**Yes, on revenue per logo. No change on how fast we add logos.**

Same Through Line baseline (48 agencies, June 2027):

| Price used | ARR at 48 agencies | Agencies needed for $1M ARR |
|---|---:|---:|
| Old concept ($299 / mo, no shopping) | **$172k** | 279 |
| Monday software only ($699, they shop themselves) | **$403k** | 119 |
| Monday full attach ($699 + $18 × 43 shops) | **$848k** | 57 |

What got better: each agency is worth enough that the ramp we already wrote is a path to $1M ARR, not a path to a few hundred thousand.

What did **not** get better: we still have to sell, onboard, and staff those 57 shops. Baseline Path to Scale already called for ~32 VAs at 48 agencies. 57 agencies at 1–1.5 agencies per VA is roughly **38–57 VAs** unless shopping is automated. Price does not hire those people.

## 6. What would make $1M slower

These are the only assumptions that really move the date.

1. **Shopping does not attach.** If they use our software and their own VAs, you need 119 logos. Baseline does not get there until early 2028 even if adds stay at +10 / month.  
2. **We comp shopping for the first logos.** Monday said that may happen. ARR looks the same on a slide; cash and real ARPU in year one are lower.  
3. **The add-rate is the conservative one.** Then $1M ARR is February 2028, not next summer.  
4. **Books are smaller than Austin’s 1,500.** A 1,000-household agency shops less. ARPU falls; you need more logos.

What does **not** move the $1M date much: TAM, the 70% personal-lines haircut, or life commission. Those change how big the pond looks, not how fast 57 logos show up.

Use **January 2028** if they mean cash in the door.
