## The two versions to build

### Version A — Base fee + per-QQ shop (default story)

Maps cleanly to JV’s “$499 platform / add shopping” gut **and** Jacob’s “$699 → $499 + $10–20/shop.”

**Narrative arc**

1. Anchor by size: list price band ~**$699–$1,499/mo** (premium/household tiers).  
2. Life discount: e.g. **$699 → $499** at JV-scale (or **$899/$1,000 → discounted**) if we handle life.  
3. “You’re already green”: 1–2% life conversion × ~$1,200 premium × agency points ≈ fee covered / in the green.  
4. Shopping: **~$15–18 per completed questionnaire** (working number; band $10–20), up to **3 carriers**, **2-business-day** turnaround.  
5. Controls: shop-offer thresholds (e.g. ≥15% increase) + **monthly shopping spend cap** (overflow to their team).  
6. Retention beat: offer-to-shop raises retention; touch everyone either way.

**What base fee includes**

- Automated renewal outreach (**every** household)  
- Questionnaire / info refresh  
- Cross-sell teeing (home/renters/umbrella — we surface, they own)  
- Life + referral asks on every touch (life fulfilled by us if discounted tier)  
- Shopping billed when QQ completes (if they take our shop)

**JV sales language you can reuse:**  
“$499 and your VAs shop — or ~$1k and we do the whole thing (still less than one VA).”  
Version A is the refined version of that (base + variable shops instead of a blunt $1k all-in).

### Version B — All-in monthly (shopping bundled)

Same story; monthly fee **includes** modeled shop volume. Use ~**43–45%** QQ rate, then threshold filter. Overage = per-shop or plan uplift.

**Why both:** See whether owners freak at **“$18 every QQ”** or prefer **one bigger predictable number**.
