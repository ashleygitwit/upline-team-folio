# Pricing

**Locked offer (as of the Monday pricing room → Aug 24 sales demo):**

> **$699 a month. $18 when we shop.** If we save you one customer a month, this pays for itself.

This is the team-facing record. The sales deck lives in [`../go-to-market/pitch-deck/`](../go-to-market/pitch-deck/). The TAM walkthrough is [`2026-08-20-path-to-1m.md`](2026-08-20-path-to-1m.md). Through Line paints the ramp on `#/scale`.

---

## The offer

| Piece | Price | What they get |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| Software | **$699 / month** | Reach every renewal, in the agent’s voice. Short questionnaire so they are not chasing updates. Cross-sell and referrals sit in the same outreach. Unlimited users. We set them up. |
| Shopping | **$18 per shop** | We run the quote work and get it back. If their team or VAs already shop, they skip this and pay $699. |

Working sales language on the shop: up to 3–4 carriers, ~24-hour turnaround (untested — watch the face). Charge when we shop, not when they switch or stay.

May credit or comp shopping for first customers. **Still show the $18 sticker.**

---

## Math we use internally

Treat a typical agency as a **1,500-household** midsize book. Members 1st questionnaire rate: **43%** → about **43 shops / month** if we do the shopping.

| | |
|---|---|
| Subscription | $699 × 12 = **$8,388** |
| Shopping | 43 × 12 × $18 = **$9,288** |
| **ARPU** | **~$17,676 / year** (~$1,473 / month) |
| Agencies for **$1M ARR** | **57** |

If they bring their own VAs and we never collect shopping, ARPU is only $8,388 and $1M ARR needs **119 agencies**. That fork matters more than $699 vs $899.

Path to Scale ramps (Through Line) hit ~$1M ARR **June 2027** aggressive / **July 2027** baseline. Cash in the bank lags ARR by months. Detail: [`2026-08-20-path-to-1m.md`](2026-08-20-path-to-1m.md).

## What is not in the first pitch

- **Life commission.** Upside is too small and unproven to hang the price on. Later extra, or drop it.
- **AMS replacement.** We want it. Not the open. Switching systems sounds painful. Bring it once they are already in Upline.

| Bundle shopping into the monthly fee | Explicitly do not. |
| Per-questionnaire billing | Discussed Aug 12; room landed on **per shop** when Upline shops. |

If they flinch on $18: anchor against a CSR’s fully loaded time (~2 hours). Do not drop to $12 on the first ask.

## Posture

Put a best-guess in market. Okay to be wrong on first customers. Price high enough to hurt a little so they make it work.

Work-session constraints (JV voice, Aug 10–12) that still inform the story: agency software ceiling around ~$500–$600/mo unless results are framed; charge for work delivered, not for switch vs stay; every renewal gets touched. Full brief: [`2026-08-12-pricing-work-session.md`](2026-08-12-pricing-work-session.md).
