# Happy path v3 (Amanda, Sept 28)

A clickable prototype of the agent's week, rebuilt from Ashley's v2 (`Ashley_v2_sept_22` on Through Line) after Ashley walked it through with Amanda and Doug. Through Line shows it as **Amanda_v3_sept_28** under Prototype.

This is a prototype, not the product. Everything is canned data in `src/data.ts` and `src/household/data.ts`, and all of it is invented: Harbor Point Insurance, Jenna Ruiz and every household were renamed on 2026-09-29, before the prototype went public on Through Line, so nothing here is a real agency, agent or policyholder. Every page carries `noindex`, and Through Line's `robots.txt` keeps crawlers out.

## Before coffee

Looking over Monday's renewals is a task agents don't do today, and we're asking them to do it before 9:00 AM on a Tuesday. So the Monday email and the homepage have to be enjoyable before the first coffee: quick, painless, and doable while checking the inbox over breakfast. A greeting, one sentence and a box to ask anything; nothing that needs thinking to find.

## What changed from v2, and why

The review's direction was fewer things on screen, said in words, one action at a time (RAMP as the north star).

- **A slate card comes before each new day, each change of person and Jenna's first sign-in.** After BrightFuture's demo (brightfuture-demo.vercel.app/2), seven cards set up what comes next, so the screen after one reads as a different day, a different place or someone else's: before the Monday email, Jenna signing in to Upline from it, Leah's inbox on Tuesday, Jenna's Wednesday and Thursday, Leah's recommendation on Thursday evening, and Jenna's Friday. Each is the stage's slate 700 with a story title in the eyebrow, the day in a chip and a sentence in the display face, fading up as it arrives. There's no rule beside the title, as BrightFuture has, because blue 600 barely shows on slate and the stage's own captions go without one. Leah's inbox-to-questionnaire runs on without a card, since she's going straight from one screen to the next. The cards' words are fixed in `src/walk.ts` and tell the usual path; if the presenter skipped the Pruitts, the screen after Tuesday's card is the one that says so. In the presenter bar they're stops like any other, and Jump to heads each group of screens with its card: pick the card to set the stage, or a screen to skip past it. The Monday email's buttons land on the sign-in card and the questionnaire's closing link on Wednesday's, while Monday's link to the questionnaire still goes straight to it.
- **The week starts with an email, not a login.** Upline writes to the agent on Monday with everything that needs them that week. It opens on the design hub homepage's band (the grained mountain on blue 600, drawn from the hub's own files) with the white logo, "Good morning, Jenna" and "Here's what needs your attention this week." Under it, the week's to-dos in the homepage's section names: **Action Needed**, one card holding everything that needs her, soonest renewal first (Rhea Iyer and Diane Mercer, approved and waiting to be bound, and Sofia Marin and Walter Kemp, shopped and ready for review), with a rule between them and one View in Upline button across the foot, in primary blue. Anything renewing inside a week says how long is left ("Renews in 4 days") rather than the date. **Scheduled Emails** comes last, as one quiet card with no button, because the six renewal emails need nothing from Jenna: outreach still sends Tuesday at 9:00 AM if she does nothing, as the strategy sprint decided. They were two sections, Closing and Shopped, until the 2026-09-29 review found the split more than it was worth.
- **The homepage opens on the same band every day, and the band only talks.** It's the design hub homepage's blue under the mountain, as the Monday email opens, and it never holds a card or a button: a greeting, one line on what's going on, the box to ask anything with three questions written out inside it (what needs me today, how's my retention, and who left), and under it a white link, "Want to review progress on your full book? View your Policyholder List.", that opens the Policyholder List. The greeting is "Good morning, Jenna", and "Happy Friday, Jenna" on Friday. The line follows the day and what the presenter has done: Monday counts what's below it ("You have four renewals that need you this week. Six renewal emails go out tomorrow at 9 AM.", fewer as she closes or skips); Wednesday says "No tasks need immediate action today. All scheduled renewal emails went out Tuesday and the Pruitts are being shopped."; Thursday, the walk's seventh stop, says "Shopping has been completed for Leah and Tom Pruitt." and, once the recommendation has gone, "Your recommendation went to Leah and Tom. We'll let you know when they answer."; Friday says "Leah and Tom said yes to Auto-Owners. Bind it in the Auto-Owners portal before November 15, then close it out from their profile below." and, once closed, "Done. The Pruitts are set." with what happens next. If the Pruitts were skipped, Thursday and Friday say "Nothing needs you today."
- **Under the band, two sections, in the Monday email's order.** **Action Needed** and **Scheduled Emails**, holding only what needs Jenna. Scheduled comes last because it goes out whether Jenna looks or not. They're drawn as the email draws them, without its buttons: each section's title sits above its card, no counts, each Action Needed task is its own card, 16 apart, and carries the name in the display face at the email's size, its lines, its carrier with the carrier's mark, its renewal (counted down inside a week) and any chips (below), and each Scheduled row has the carrier's mark beside the name and, on Monday, the same three-dot menu as an Action Needed card after the change. Action Needed is soonest renewal first. A shopped row opens on a preview of what the shop came back with, on the left the way the design hub's template cards set their picture (a results pick list in miniature, each carrier's mark and price with the pick selected), and ends its note with View the full report; the whole row opens the results. A closing row says what they approved and ends in View profile, which opens the drawer, where Close out lives: it had a memo field and Mark as Closed until the 2026-09-29 review, where Austin pointed out that closing is a morning in the carrier's portal and on the phone, not a field on the homepage. Once closed out the row says so with the note, and Undo reopens it. Every Action Needed card's three-dot menu has View Profile, Snooze (below) and Report an Error; a Scheduled line's has View Profile and Report an Error. View Renewal History came out of every menu on 2026-09-30, since Recent activity in the drawer already covers it. On Monday, as on Ashley's v2 board, Action Needed holds Rhea Iyer and Diane Mercer, approved and waiting to be bound, and Sofia Marin and Walter Kemp, whose recommendations are ready to send. On Thursday it holds the Pruitts' shop (Home + Auto · Erie · Renews Nov 15, "Auto-Owners came in at $4,640 for the same coverage, $1,050 less than Erie's renewal. View the full report"), which opens their shop results in a modal, reached by clicking rather than being a stop of its own, and on Friday their approval (the chat's Mark it done closes it out too). On Monday Scheduled has the email's sentence and the six, one line each with the carrier's mark, the name and the change; a line opens that household's outreach review straight away, on its own (below). Later in the week it has the nudges and follow-ups that go on their own, each with why it's going. A section with nothing in it that day isn't drawn: Wednesday is Scheduled alone.
- **Snooze puts a task off, and a quiet line keeps it in sight.** Austin's ask at the 2026-09-29 review, as the first step toward task management: every Action Needed row's menu, and the clock at the end of the drawer's blue banner, offer Until tomorrow, Until next week and Until 5 days before renewal (the last isn't offered once the renewal is inside five days). A snoozed task leaves the list and shows in a Snoozed line at the foot of the section, with Undo; the drawer's banner turns gray and says "Snoozed until next week." with Undo; Recent activity logs it; and the household wears a Snoozed chip. Tomorrow's snooze is over at the next stop, next week's outlasts the walk, and the renewal's lets go five days before it (`src/tasks.ts`). It's kept in the walk, so it survives stepping back and forth.
- **Chips say what's pinned to a household, wherever it appears.** On Action Needed rows, in the drawer's header, and on the Policyholder board and list (`src/components/Chips.tsx`). **Life quote requested** is the blue one, since it asks something of Jenna: Sofia Marin has it from her questionnaire the week before, and the Pruitts from Wednesday if Leah said yes on Tuesday (her answer is the walk's). **Info updated** is gray: Neha Rao from Monday, the Pruitts from Wednesday. Behind it, Recent activity says how many details changed ("1 detail changed on file"), and in Details each changed row carries a pencil whose tooltip says to update it in EZLynx, the agency's system of record, which Upline can't write to yet. **Snoozed** is above. Both new chips came out of the 2026-09-29 review, where the cross-sell and the data-hygiene paths turned out to run on their own timelines and neither has a flow of its own yet.
- **A household opens in one drawer, under the navigation rather than over it.** It started as the drawer Ashley's board opens (v2.5's `screens/queue/HouseholdSheet.tsx`, ported into `src/household/` with her household data), and every household now gets the same one: the stage over the name, the name and the change, a banner when something is going on, and three tabs, Details, Recent activity and Notes, which always open on Details. It opens from a row menu's View Profile, from a closing row's View profile and from the Policyholder List (a Scheduled row opens the outreach review on its own instead), and all twelve follow the walk's day (`src/household/activity.ts`): this week's six from Monday's email through the week's statuses and nudges, and the earlier weeks' six from Ashley's board on Monday to the Policyholder List's stages after. The banner is uplineinsurance.com's founding-member banner, blue when it needs Jenna (results to review, an approval to bind) and gray when it only says what's going on (the renewal email or a nudge scheduled, a shop running, a recommendation out); when nothing is going on there's no banner, and a hairline stands in its place. It opens that phase's modal, as Review always has, so the walk's clicks are the same. **Recent activity** is a timeline, newest first, under Up next (the scheduled email or nudge, and the renewal date). Most lines are a line of text, with a link where there's a modal behind them; what needs Jenna (the results came back, or they approved) is a card with a button, on the rail's one blue dot. The time has its own column on a wide drawer and sits over the line on a phone. **Notes** opens empty. A note goes in from the box at the foot (Add note, or Cmd or Ctrl + Enter) and lands above it with Jenna's avatar, the walk's day and the clock time, and it stays for the rest of the walk.
- **Each phase's work opens in a modal, with its buttons in the footer.** They're all drawn the same way (`src/household/phases.tsx`): the drawer's header and ground, the work scrolling under it, and the footer under the scroll. The modal is wider and shorter than the drawer, 800 by at most 640, so it reads as a dialog over the page rather than a second drawer floating over the first (Ashley's note at the 2026-09-29 review). The outreach review opens on its own from a Scheduled row on Monday, as the shop results do from Thursday's row, because reviewing the email is the row's whole point and shouldn't sit behind the drawer; from the drawer's banner and Recent activity it opens over the drawer as the rest do (`src/household/Outreach.tsx` wires it either way). Once their action is taken they open read-only, with the footer saying what happened. The renewal email's review is the policy, the email and Shape the shop, a row of on-and-off buttons (Homeowners, Auto, Medicare and Life) with what doesn't apply grayed and saying why on hover, over Skip outreach and Send now. The email's "Answer a few quick questions" is a link: the Pruitts' goes to Leah's questionnaire in the walk, as if it opened in another tab, and the others say only the Pruitts' questionnaire is in this prototype. The close-out says what's left, asks what happened, and Close out keeps the answer as the household's memo. What happens in them lands in the walk: the email Jenna edits is the one Leah gets, the Life button decides the questionnaire's life question, Send now marks the email as looking good or sends the nudge (which takes it off the homepage's Scheduled list), Skip skips it and Undo brings it back, sending a recommendation moves the household to Rec sent, and Close out closes it, the Pruitts' the same as the chat's Mark it done.

## Brand and components

- Tokens are copied from the design hub (`treadwell-investor-deck`, `src/app/globals.css`): one blue (`#082db1`), no corner radius, gray/200 hairlines, Radio Canada / Radio Canada Big / Reddit Mono. If the brand moves, re-copy them rather than editing here.
- Components are shadcn's `radix-vega` style, matching the Upline shadcncraft Base 3.1.0 library. The ones the hub already had (button, badge, item, sheet, field, input, textarea, label, separator, tooltip) are the hub's own Upline-mode files; the rest follow the same rules.
- Policyholder-facing screens are Harbor Point's, not Upline's: `.agency` in `src/index.css` swaps `--primary` for the agency's green, so every component inside follows.

## Placeholders to know about

- The chat is canned. Each suggested question plays a written answer, and a typed question goes to the question it sounds most like, or gets told there's no answer yet. Three more questions answer when typed (how much have I saved my clients, who's asked about a life quote, where does everyone stand), and "See where the rest stand" on Thursday asks the last one. A day's chat lasts for the walk and is gone on reload.
- Sofia Marin, Walter Kemp, Rhea Iyer and Diane Mercer come from Ashley's v2 with the details it gave them. Their rows don't open anything, since only the Pruitts have results; in each of their rows' menus View Profile opens the drawer, while Report an Error closes the menu and goes nowhere yet, and the View the full report link on Sofia's and Walter's is drawn but not built and says so when pointed at, and Jenna clears them Monday afternoon off-camera, which is why they're gone by Wednesday.
- Profile and Account Settings in Jenna's menu close the menu and go nowhere yet. On the Policyholder List, the three who left have no file, so their rows don't open and say so when pointed at, and their premium and contact show a dash. Neha's and Marcus's results aren't in the prototype, so after Monday their drawer's banner says the recommendation went but opens nothing.
- After Monday, Scheduled Emails lists the nudges and follow-ups the statuses mention, one line each. Their emails (`nudges` in `src/data.ts`) open from the household's drawer, not from the homepage's line.
- Carrier logos are text, apart from the Monday email, which puts each carrier's mark beside its name in the carrier's own colors. It follows the design hub's CarrierMark: only the marks uplineinsurance.com already ships (`upline-marketing-site/public/logos`) are used, and that set has Nationwide and Travelers. Erie, Westfield, Auto-Owners, Grange and Ohio Mutual show their initial on gray until sanctioned color files are added to `src/assets/carriers`.
- The homepage's lists have no pictures, only the carrier's mark, apart from the shopped rows' previews, which are drawn in code from each household's quotes (Sofia's and Walter's from Ashley's household data) rather than being screenshots.
- The retention strip is the one bespoke graphic; the library's Progress is the fallback.
- The +4 points against last year may not be a number we can get from agencies. The three households who left, and their stories, are invented.
- Snooze has no clock behind it: the three choices are worked out against the walk's four days (`src/tasks.ts`), and "Until next week" simply outlasts the walk. Which details the questionnaire changed is written down per household there too, not derived from the answers.
- Desktop only.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Opens on port 5310. The presenter bar at the top steps through the sixteen stops; the arrow keys do the same.

## Publish it to Through Line

```bash
npm run export
```

Builds one self-contained HTML file (fonts included, no network needed) into `internal-comms/public/prototype-v3.html`.

## Capture it for the feedback board

With the dev server running:

```bash
npm run capture
```

Writes every screen and state (the sixteen stops, and everything that opens from each: menus, the drawer's tabs and banners, the phase modals, the Policyholder board and table, the chat) into `captures/` as PNGs, 1440 wide at 2×, with the presenter bar cropped off and every scrolling region opened out to its full height, plus a `manifest.json` saying what each one is and how it was reached. It drives the Chrome already on the Mac through Playwright, so there's no browser download. `captures/` is ignored by git; the PNGs live on the FigJam board (Upline Design Feedback V3, the "Full Prototype Walk Through" page). `npm run capture 12` recaptures only the slugs starting with `12`.

`node scripts/figjam-layout.mjs <folder>` turns the manifest into the Figma Plugin API code that lays the board out, one file per stop, for running through the Figma MCP's `use_figma`; each file returns its image frames' ids, which `upload_assets` fills with the PNGs. The list of states is `captures` in `scripts/capture.mjs`; add a state there when the prototype grows one.
