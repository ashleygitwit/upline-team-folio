## How shopping runs on the MVP map

Upline picks the carriers. The VA does not choose.

1. Customer opts into shop on the questionnaire
2. Upline locks **three** appointed carriers and opens a ticket
3. VA claims the ticket, shops those three portals (~30–45 min), attaches quote PDFs
4. Upline reads the PDFs and drafts the recommendation
5. Agent reviews, edits, sends

**This stays a person at launch.** Auto-shopping is a later Path to Scale pace (roughly spring), not the MVP line.

VA also deep-pulls **this week’s ~30** in EZLynx before outreach (History, FPD, docs, notes). That is the same pre-work Members 1st taught us — just scoped to the week, not the whole book.

## Cost we use when we talk about “can we run this”

Assumptions on the Through Line MVP page (one ~1,500-household agency):

| | |
|---|---|
| VA rate | $8/hr fully loaded |
| Shops | ~8–9 / week · ~38 min |
| Shopping labor | ~$190 / month |
| Pre-outreach refresh | ~$65 / month (→ ~$0 if RPA does it) |
| RPA (initial load + weekly renewal pulls) | ~$20–100 / month |
| Close | $0 to Upline (agency servicing team) |
| **Recurring total** | **~$350–430 / month per agency** |

That is run-cost, not price. We charge $699 + $18/shop. See [`pricing.md`](pricing.md).

## What “shop” means when we sell it

- Agency / their VAs shop → they pay $699 only
- Upline shops → $18 each
- Up to 3–4 carriers, the right ones for the household
- Sales language: ~24-hour turnaround or you do not pay (untested)

Do not bundle shopping into the monthly fee. Do not put AMS Desk or shop-bundle SKUs back on the offer.
