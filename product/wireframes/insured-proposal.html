How I looked at this

Seabrook has access to fourteen carriers for households like yours. I did not send your family to all fourteen, and I’d be suspicious of any agent who told you they had. Most of them were never going to write a home with a 2004 roof, a four-year-old German shepherd, and a hail claim on the file — and the ones that would have wanted to exclude something that matters to you.

I picked three carriers that could actually win your business, put the real details of your household in front of them, and read what came back. One of them I’m recommending. Two I ruled out, and I’ve written down why below, including the one that came back cheaper.

Donegal — your current carrier
