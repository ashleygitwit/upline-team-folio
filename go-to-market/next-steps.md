## Paid social — the v1 launch question

**Recommendation: yes to end of next week, but point it at a standalone landing page rather than
waiting on the website.**

The reasoning is calendar, not quality. Tuesday's read was that instrumentation is the hard part and
the ask was to get the machine crawling in about a month. That is the whole game here — feeding the
algorithms which videos become demos is a learning loop, and the loop cannot start until something
is live. Launch on Sept 18 and paid is producing real demo flow by mid-to-late October, leaving
about six useful weeks against the end-of-November number. Slip to the end of September and paid
contributes almost nothing to the metric it exists to move, because the loop never gets enough
cycles. Rough and live beats polished and late.

**But there is a dependency nobody has named: paid social currently depends on the website, and both
are scheduled for the same day.** Tuesday was explicit that if we send people to the site from ads,
we have to feel very good about the story and the CTA. If website v2 lands end of next week and paid
launches end of next week, paid is pointing at something that does not exist yet.

**The way out is the page we are already building.** The conference QR page is a single-purpose
landing page with a demo CTA. Build paid v1 against a sibling page using the same pattern, and paid
stops depending on website v2 entirely. Two benefits beyond unblocking: we get a clean read on ad
performance without the whole site as a confound, and Claire gets to ship v2 on quality rather than
on a date set by someone else's launch.

**The real blocker is probably creative, not destination.** The sprint goal was to leave last week
with the first 20 videos recorded. If those do not exist, that is the thing to fix Monday — targeting
and pixels are a day of work, and footage is not.

**Also worth flagging: Austin owns this lane and is at the SIA conference next week**, presenting on
one day. Either the launch is front-loaded to Monday and Tuesday, or someone covers the launch
mechanics while he is out.

**Next steps for the Austin working session:**

- [ ] Confirm how many videos actually exist, and what the gap is to a testable set
- [ ] Decide destination: standalone landing page (recommended) or wait for website v2
- [ ] Stand up tracking end to end — pixel, UTMs, and demo-booked as the conversion event, not clicks
- [ ] Pick the first platform to spend on. Tuesday named LinkedIn, Meta, and Google Display; do not start on all three
- [ ] Set a weekly spend cap and a decision rule for killing a creative
- [ ] Name the fallback owner for the days Austin is at SIA

**Already decided, do not relitigate:** paid video is the swing play, not influencer. Cold outbound
continues alongside it. Do not pull Doug off product to build this.

**Fun mechanic from Tuesday that should not get lost:** everyone on the team creates at least one ad,
we track click-through by author, and the winner gets a bike or an equivalent gift card. Submit as
many as you want.
