# Go-to-Market

Sales and GTM motions for Upline — demos, prospect conversations, and sales-facing pitch materials.

This is one of the four top-level folders in Team Folio (alongside [`../internal-comms/`](../internal-comms/), [`../project-planning/`](../project-planning/), and [`../product/`](../product/)).

## What belongs here

- **Demo apps** used on sales / waitlist calls (e.g. `agent-demo/`)
- **Sales demo transcripts and notes** from prospect conversations (`sales-demos/`)
- **Sales decks** — the thing we put in front of a buyer (`pitch-deck/`)

## What does *not* belong here

- Pricing model, journey map, VA/shopping, MVP line → [`../product/`](../product/)
- Sprint week and timeline notes → [`../project-planning/`](../project-planning/)
- Through Line pages → [`../internal-comms/`](../internal-comms/)
- Pilot design-customer learnings → those stay in the separate [upline-poc](https://github.com/austinboardman/upline-poc) repo

Pricing work that used to sit in `pitch-deck/` (the Aug 12 work session and the path-to-1M memo) now lives in [`../product/`](../product/). The Aug 24 sales demo stays here.

## Source labeling

When promoting a quote or insight from here into the Folio site (`data/learnings.json`) or other product docs, always tag the source as **sales demo** (vs. **pilot**) so the streams stay distinct.
