# Go-to-Market

Sales and GTM motions for Upline — demos, prospect conversations, and pitch materials.

This is one of the two primary content folders in Team Folio (alongside [`../venture/`](../venture/)).

## What belongs here

- **Demo apps** used on sales / waitlist calls (e.g. `agent-demo/`)
- **Sales demo transcripts and notes** from prospect conversations (`sales-demos/`)
- Pitch decks, outreach experiments, and other GTM artifacts as they show up

## What does *not* belong here

- Venture / product strategy narrative → [`../venture/`](../venture/)
- Pilot design-customer learnings → those stay in the separate [upline-poc](https://github.com/austinboardman/upline-poc) repo

## Source labeling

When promoting a quote or insight from here into the Folio site (`data/learnings.json`) or other venture docs, always tag the source as **sales demo** (vs. **pilot**) so the streams stay distinct.
