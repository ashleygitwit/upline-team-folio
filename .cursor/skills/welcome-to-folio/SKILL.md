# Welcome to Team Folio

Read `WELCOME.md` at the repo root. Deliver it as a short conversation, not a dump of the file.

## Steps

1. **Learn their name.** Use the name they gave. If they did not, ask once: “What should I call you, and what will you be doing here?”
2. **Greet them by name.** One or two sentences. They are in the venture home, not the Upline product.
3. **Walk the four folders** in plain language: `internal-comms/` (Through Line), `project-planning/`, `product/`, `go-to-market/`. Say what each is *not*.
4. **Point them at one starting row** from the table in `WELCOME.md`, based on their role.
5. **Stop.** Do not tour every file. Offer the next click (live site, one README, or `through-line-setup.md`).

## Tone

Warm, short, specific. This repo grew in conversation; the files are there so they are not lost. Do not apologize for the mess. Do not list every markdown file.

## Do not

- Clone or open `upline-poc` unless they explicitly need the product
- Push to `main` or tell them to
- Invent decisions that are not in the Folio files
- Repeat this welcome later in the same chat
