# Strategy sprint — week of September 8, 2026

**Status:** Locked plan as of late August. Through Line page: `#/sprint`.  
**What this is:** Translation week, not discovery. We are not starting from zero.

Three days **in the room**, Monday through Wednesday. Thursday and Friday are company-wide — no Upline work those days. The week after, write a short build spec. MVP build target: **Nov 6**.

## Product days

### Monday — Lock the bet, then turn it into a path

- **Morning.** Evidence. Pressure-test the bet. Define the 90-day win. Draw the MVP line — above the line vs on ice.
- **Afternoon.** Walk the drafted journey and rebuild it together.

Rule for the week: if the bet is wrong, change the bet. If the experience does not serve the bet, change the experience. Do not do both at once.

### Tuesday — Make the three moments concrete

- **Morning.** Pick the three most important touchpoints. Breadboard each: what lives here, what someone can do, what decision it supports.
- **Afternoon.** Ashley + Amanda sketch. Austin + engineer work logistics, data, feasibility against the same three moments. End of day: hold or revise.

### Wednesday — Heads-down, make the experience legible

- Ashley + Amanda on wires and visual direction.
- Austin + engineer on research, logistics, onboarding.
- Reviews stay sparse. Leave with enough to write the spec the following week.

---

## By Wednesday night

- A product bet we either held or revised
- 90-day success criteria
- A clear above-the-line MVP scope and an on-ice list
- A shared user journey map
- Three breadboarded touchpoints
- First-pass wires and design direction

The week after: a short build spec — what to build, how we know it is done, what is in, what is on ice. Readable by an engineer and a designer. Not a hundred tickets.

**Order of work:** journey first → features second → screens third → spec last.
