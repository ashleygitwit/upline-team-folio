## The call on how we run the room

Do **not** build a Google Design Sprint deck and reconstruct the week after.

| Job | What we use | What we do not use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People know where we are | A **today strip** (printed, or one slide on a TV): day, block, the question, the clock | A 40-slide walkthrough of every step |
| People do the work | **The wall.** Printed strawman, bet, value-adds, empty 2×2. Stickies on paper. | Live typing into Folio / Notion / FigJam as the main surface |
| You know what to say next | **Facilitation cards** — one half-sheet per block with the method and the timeboxes | Reading the Notion doc off a laptop |
| Yesterday becomes today | **Nightly 45 min in this repo** → a one-pager you put on the wall at 9:00 | Rebuilding a deck from memory at 11pm |

Through Line is the **pre-read** and the **next-morning recap**. It is not the facilitation tool.

## The call on capture

The wall is the record during the day. The transcript is the backup. The nightly pass is synthesis — not archaeology.

**In the room**

1. One laptop on the table, recording from 9:00 (Granola, Otter, or a Zoom “recording buddy”). Do not rely on a phone in a bag.
2. **Phase capture sheets** — one printed page per strawman phase. You or Amanda fills as you walk: hold / revise / drop, risk, unknown, flagship, sell / do not promise.
3. Photograph the wall at every close. Tuesday 4:10 is already on the agenda. Do the same Wednesday 3:00, Thursday noon, Friday noon.

**That night (45–60 min, you + Cursor in Folio)**

Drop in: the transcript, the wall photos, the capture sheets (photo is fine). Ask for:

- What we **held / revised / dropped**
- The flagships, the risk we cannot ignore, the unknowns
- Tomorrow’s **one-pager** — ten lines, printable
- Any Through Line update that is actually true now

**Next morning (5 min)**

Put yesterday’s one-pager on the wall. Read it. That is how we “present” yesterday. Then start today’s block.

Do not live-edit Folio in front of the room. Do not wait until Friday to organize.
